## 3. Strict Non-Fabrication Rules

1. **No Hallucinated Years:** A question is marked with an examination session only when that specific topic appeared in the official WBSCTE diploma paper.
2. **Transparent Practice Labeling:** Questions formulated to provide comprehensive syllabus coverage that did not appear in the four uploaded papers are explicitly labeled as **Important Practice Question** (`type: 'Practice'`).
3. **Multi-Year Merging:** When a question appeared across multiple exam sessions (e.g., `iot-u1-lq2` in Mar 2023, Jan 2024, Dec 2024, and Jan 2026), all years are displayed on the card in chronological order.
